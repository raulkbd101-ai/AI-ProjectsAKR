How I work

From strategic question to inspectable artifact.

The projects turn familiar enterprise concerns into compact demos: define the decision, make constraints explicit, build a visible workflow, test the rules, and state what the prototype does not establish.

1

Frame the business decision

Clarify what a leader, operator, or reviewer needs to understand and what evidence would support the decision.

2

Make policy and assumptions visible

Use clear scopes, deterministic rules, synthetic data, and explicit human review points.

3

Build, run, and verify

Keep each example easy to launch locally, cover important behavior with tests, and capture what actually ran.

4

Explain the limits and next decision

Separate a working prototype from production readiness, measured business outcomes, and real-world authorization.

About this portfolio
These are self-directed learning projects, not employer or client deployments. I use AI coding assistance during implementation. My work here is to select the problem and constraints, review the design, run and validate the examples, and communicate the evidence and limitations. No synthetic outcome is presented as a business result.
